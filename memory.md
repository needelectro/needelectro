## 2. Core Vision

NEED ELECTRO is being developed as an integrated electrical and
technological ecosystem rather than only a conventional electrical
service business.

The platform connects:

-   Electrical services
-   Technology products
-   Computer and laptop products
-   CCTV and security
-   Smart electrical solutions
-   Technician services
-   Product warranty
-   Customer support
-   Training
-   Employment
-   Business technology
-   ERP integration

## 3. Primary Customer Model

Customers should be able to create one account and use the platform for:

-   Services
-   Products
-   Orders
-   Bookings
-   Payments
-   Warranty
-   Support
-   Reviews
-   Service history

## 4. Technician Model

Technicians can independently create accounts.

They submit:

-   Identity information
-   Professional information
-   Skills
-   Experience
-   Certificates
-   Service areas
-   Availability

They must be verified before accepting jobs.

Verified technicians can:

-   View available jobs
-   Accept jobs
-   Complete jobs
-   Update job status
-   Earn money
-   View wallet transactions
-   Receive ratings
-   Build work history

## 5. Service Model

Core services include:

-   Complete electrical work
-   House wiring
-   Electrical installation
-   Electrical troubleshooting
-   Light and fan installation
-   IPS/UPS installation
-   Solar electrical solutions
-   Smart electrical solutions
-   CCTV installation and setup
-   Computer services
-   Laptop services
-   Networking
-   Technical maintenance
-   Emergency electrical services

Service categories must remain configurable from admin.

## 6. Product Model

Core product categories include:

-   Laptops
-   Computers
-   Computer accessories
-   CCTV
-   Networking
-   Electrical products
-   Smart devices
-   Gadgets
-   UPS/IPS
-   Solar products
-   Other technology products

Products should support stock, SKU, images, specifications, warranty,
and serial numbers where appropriate.

## 7. ERP Relationship

NEED ELECTRO ERP is a separate but connected business management system.

The digital platform must be ERP-ready.

Important future synchronization areas:

-   Customers
-   Products
-   Inventory
-   Sales
-   Orders
-   Bookings
-   Technicians
-   Warranty
-   Payments
-   Cash
-   Expenses
-   Invoices
-   Reports

The platform should not create an incompatible financial architecture.

## 8. PWA

The main website should also function as a Progressive Web App.

There should initially be one shared web/PWA codebase rather than
separate duplicated applications.

A dedicated native technician application may be introduced later if
advanced device capabilities require it.

## 9. Current Brand Positioning

Current public brand material presents NEED ELECTRO through:

-   Laptops, Computers, CCTV and Computer Accessories
-   Complete electrical work of the house
-   Safe and modern smart electric solutions
-   CCTV camera installation and setup services

The new platform should expand this identity rather than contradict it.

## 10. Geographic Direction

Initial market:

Moulvibazar and surrounding service areas.

Long-term direction:

Expand across Bangladesh.

The architecture must support multiple branches, service areas,
technicians, inventory locations, and regional administration.

## 15. Source of Truth

The project repository should treat these documents as living
specifications.

When implementation and documentation conflict:

1.  Identify the conflict.
2.  Determine the intended current business rule.
3.  Update the appropriate documentation.
4.  Then implement the change.

Do not silently create contradictory behavior.

## 16. Current Principle

Build the foundation correctly before scaling.

The platform must be capable of starting locally in Moulvibazar while
having the technical structure required for long-term Bangladesh-wide
expansion.
